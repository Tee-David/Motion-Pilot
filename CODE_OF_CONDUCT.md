# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Motion Pilot to be a welcoming, professional and constructive open-source project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to treat one another with respect regardless of experience level, background, identity or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Critique work, not people, and give feedback someone can act on. Assume good intent while still reviewing rigorously, accept corrections gracefully, keep discussions relevant to the project, and protect private or sensitive information you come across, including clients' unreleased work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information or unreleased work, or other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Reports should be made privately to the maintainer rather than through a public issue when the report itself contains sensitive information.

## Scope

This code applies in Motion Pilot project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.
