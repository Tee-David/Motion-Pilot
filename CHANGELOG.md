# Changelog

All notable changes to Motion Pilot are documented here. The project follows Semantic Versioning.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added

- `SKILL.md`: the steering document, with Film, Web motion and Review modes, the house taste, principles as numbers, the tells of generated motion, a decide-twice process and the engine contract
- The brief: materials offered as a menu, where to find references, voice and music options, variants, and plain language for non-specialists
- Process references: storylines (devices and forms), the SPEC, storyboards, measured review with a defect table and ranked notes, delivery
- Craft references: principles in animator's terms, timing and easing, transitions with a cause, type in motion, logo construction, composition, sound, voice, copywriting
- Ten genre playbooks: brand film, product demo, explainer, launch ad, logo reveal, web motion, hero loop, data story, kinetic type, showreel
- A 33-reference style library with measured pacing, palette and loudness, and a ledger of lessons from approved work
- Engine: deterministic scenes, shared cubic-bezier curves by role, a damping spring, perceptual scale, posterise; components and three templates
- Scripts: brand probe, reference analysis with change events and holds, project scaffold with facts, storylines and spec files, font fetch, render, synthesised sound, voice alignment and time maps, ElevenLabs and local voices, QC and review
- The Motion Pilot logo: a paper plane at the end of a dotted loop-the-loop, with light, dark and stacked lockups, an outlined Space Grotesk wordmark and a favicon (`assets/logo/`)
- Worked examples: a voice-led social reel and two shipped preloaders with page transitions
