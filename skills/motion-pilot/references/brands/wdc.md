# We Dig Creativity (wedigcreativity.com.ng)
A creative studio: branding and design, SEO, websites, apps, software and AI, social and ads. Always "SEO", never "Search". "We Dig Creativity" means the studio loves creativity: never literal digging (no spades, earth or tunnels). No city names in copy.

## Tokens
Navy `#000065` (brand ground, ink on paper) · night `#08081f` (dark sheet) · paper `#f9f7f4` · orange `#ff6500` (the cursor, the nib, the reveal ring, never words) · orange that carries words `#b84a00` with white · mute `#9d9ec9` (trails, a name's first state) · ink `#0e0e2c`. Space Grotesk 700 for display (-0.025 to -0.035em), Outfit 400 to 500 for support. The mark is a C made of two arcs held by a hand, with a pen nib at its centre; the lockup's wordmark sits on two lines ("We Dig / Creativity") at 0.45 of the mark's height with a gap of 0.22. Mark centre in its 904 × 944 viewBox: 515, 459.

## Motion identity
- Personality: confident and clean.
- Signature curve: `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` for arrivals; `(.4,0,1,1)` for exits; `(.77,0,.18,1)` for sheets.
- Duration palette: 200 / 460 / 680 ms.
- Signature gesture: **the orange cursor is the protagonist.** It types the hook, wipes between scenes, and drops into the mark to become the pen nib. In the preloaders the hand draws its own C.
- Grounds: light is paper and navy ink with real shadows; dark is a lifted night with white ink, or the blueprint navy with hairline guides. Orange only for the cursor, the nib and one ring.

## Voice
Cocky, cheeky, never insulting. An approved structure: a dare ("You'll give this ad five seconds. Your customers? Even less.") → the named pain (designer, developer, SEO guy, social media manager: "one big headache") → "So… fire them all. Okay… maybe keep your accountant." → "So what if yours was the brand they clock instantly?" with six benefit lines → "Still here? Good. We're not for everyone." → "We Dig Creativity. It's unfair… That's the point." Voice: female American, ElevenLabs v3 (the approved take is 42.6 s).

## Approved and rejected
- Hooks centred and on one line; service titles on one line at a shared size; no orange glow in corners.
- Phones show real apps (client dashboards and listings), never website pages; a product is not repeated across three phones.
- Hero films are muted with almost no type: the page supplies the headline and a chapter bar (The studio 0:00, Branding 0:11, SEO 0:13, Web 0:15, Apps 0:17, Software and AI 0:19, Social and ads 0:21, The proof 0:23). Desktop action in the right half, phone action in the top third. The light film is a pure white ground with no veil.
- Preloaders (2026-10-25): Ink (draw and gather) is the default, Blueprint (construct) the alternative, chosen in the site's settings. The name types without the cursor riding it; Blueprint parts with no orange seam; on phones the Ink curtain drops from the top and lifts back up; the menu holds still under it.
- Hero motion pieces: every piece has light and dark grounds; loops end on holds; pieces share one ground for hand-offs.
- Rejected directions: anything with literal digging; the cream-paper-serif look; additive glow on paper.

## Deliverables pattern
Social 16:9 and 9:16 with voice (dark and light), hero desktop and phone muted loops (dark and light), posters, chapter timestamps for the developer. Recreated client UIs had placeholder figures: confirm real numbers before reuse.
