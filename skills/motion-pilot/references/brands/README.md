# Brand profiles

One file per brand, named for it (`acme.md`). Read it before any work for that brand, and update it at the end of each piece with what was approved and what was rejected, so the next piece starts informed. Profiles of client brands are private working notes: keep them out of anything you publish.

## Template

```markdown
# <Brand> (<site>)
One line: what they do, for whom.

## Tokens
Colours with roles (ground, ink, accent and where it may appear, mute); fonts with weights and tracking;
logo files (SVG paths), lockups, the icon alone, the mark's centre of rotation.

## Motion identity
- Personality: calm | premium | confident | friendly | energetic
- Signature curve: cubic-bezier(...)
- Duration palette: quick / standard / slow in ms
- Signature gesture: the one move only this brand owns
- Grounds: light and dark, and how each is built (ink and shadow, or white and rim light)

## Voice
Tone, structures that were approved, words to avoid, pronunciations.

## Claims policy
What may be said, what needs confirmation, regulated limits.

## Approved and rejected
Dated lines: what was approved, what was corrected and why.

## Assets
Where the real screens, photos and work live; what was recreated.
```
