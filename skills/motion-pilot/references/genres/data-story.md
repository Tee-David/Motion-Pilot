# Numbers, charts and data stories

## One number at a time

- A number is earned by the scene before it, then made physical: a count that becomes a skyline, a share that becomes a crowd.
- The number is the biggest thing on screen, in tabular figures, counting up over 1.2 to 1.6 s on a long ease-out, then holding.
- Real data only, from `facts.md`, rounded the same way everywhere, with a small source line on screen. One data module drives every figure, so the numbers agree with each other.

## Charts as pictures

- Build a chart in reading order: the frame and axis, a reference line, the data drawing in, then one annotation on the point that matters. Annotate on the mark; no legends.
- Bars grow from the baseline; lines draw left to right on a both-ways ease; gauges sweep.
- Highlight one series, grey the rest. A chart with one thing to say says it in its title.
- The same object carries across beats: a bar becomes the ground of the next scene, a line becomes the road the camera follows.

## Tells

A grid of KPI tiles; a dashboard; a pie chart; every number counting at once; numbers with no source.
