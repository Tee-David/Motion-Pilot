# Launch films, social ads and reels

A launch or social piece is judged in its first second and watched muted more often than not. It has to work as a picture before it works as a sentence.

## The first second

- Frame 0 is the thumbnail and the hook: a face, the result, or one bold line already on screen. Never black, never the logo alone.
- The change or the question arrives in the first beat. A hit or a click in the first 0.5 s.
- The picture carries the hook with the sound off; speech is captioned or anchored on screen.

## Shape

| Length | Beats |
| --- | --- |
| 6 s | Hook, payoff, mark |
| 15 s | Hook, one promise, one proof, call to action |
| 30 s | Hook, tension, reveal, proof (three verbs), payoff, call to action |
| 45 to 60 s | A voice-led story or a launch montage: a new world every one to two seconds, a bookend ending |

## Launch montage grammar

Measured on a strong 62 s launch film in the library (R26): the ground switches between black, white and the brand colour every beat; one object is carried across cuts (a cube drops into a wave, the wave's peak sends out rings, the rings become a pill); titles arrive per word with a short blur, the key word in the brand colour; coins gather in a ring and implode into a streak that becomes the hero coin on a sunburst; sticker type with a tucked second word; glass UI pills for features; a typed web address in a search pill near the end; the closing shot repeats the opening motif. About 3.7 sound onsets a second at −13.5 LUFS, the music building to the payoff.

## Vertical (9:16)

- Made at 1080 × 1920 and recomposed, never squeezed from 16:9.
- Words between 168 px and 1330 px from the top; nothing under the right-hand action rail; below 1330 px only texture.
- One subject, centred, stacked; landscape layouts rebuilt as a vertical sequence.
- Type about 1.35× the landscape size; at most five English words a line for a claim; cut words, never shrink.
- Shots of 1 to 2 s, a new beat every 2 to 4 s, the payoff before the swipe.

## Copy

Calm, specific, a little cheeky where the brand allows it. Aim provocation at the situation, never at the viewer. No guarantees; no invented numbers. One call to action, said and shown the same way.

## Tells

Every cut a spin or zoom; the same whoosh on each; a feature list as three cards; captions that cover the product; a logo in the corner the whole time.
