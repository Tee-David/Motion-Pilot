# Brand films

A brand film says who a company is without listing what it sells. It succeeds when the viewer can name the feeling and remembers one image. Usually 30 to 75 s, voice-led or music-led, in 16:9 and 9:16, both themes when it may live on a website.

## Find the spine

- One idea the brand stands for, in the viewer's words. "Six briefs, one studio." "Clarity in every number." Not a slogan list.
- One carrier the eye follows from the first shot to the last: a cursor that types the hook and becomes the nib of the logo; a listing card that becomes the window of the mark; a thread, a line, a single orange dot.
- A before and after told as scenes: the scattered way (vendors in a cloud of chips, a desk of spreadsheets) churning, the brand's way moving calmly.

## Shape (about 40 s, voice-led)

| Beat | Time | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dare or question | 0 to 4 s | A line that makes the viewer lean in, spoken and anchored on screen in a few words |
| The mess | 4 to 10 s | The problem as a picture: many things, too many voices, a cloud that churns |
| The turn | 10 to 13 s | The carrier gathers the mess into one point; a joke or a beat of silence |
| What changes | 13 to 30 s | Real work or real product in four to six beats, one per service or promise, each in its own world |
| The proof | 30 to 35 s | Real clients, real output; never invented numbers |
| Close | 35 to 40 s | The mark constructed, the name, the line, held 2 s or more |

## Treatments that work

- **Worlds change**: the ground changes every one or two beats (dark, light, brand colour), always through a cause.
- **Bookends**: the closing shot rhymes with the opening (the same ring, the same motif resolved), which also makes a muted loop seamless.
- **Work as a gallery in motion**: the client's real work on cards that tilt, gather into a wall and part again, rather than a grid of thumbnails.
- **Type as anchors**: short words on screen at the key spoken moments (the hook, each service name, the close); images between them.

## Light and dark

Build the film from tokens so the light version is a re-theme, not a re-edit: paper and ink with real shadows instead of a navy night with rim light. Level the light version to a true white ground when it sits on a white page.

## Tells

A montage of stock clips; the mission statement read over a gradient; three cards of values; a logo that fades in at the end; music that never changes.
