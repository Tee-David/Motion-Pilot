# Showreels and work films

A showreel sells range and taste at once. The work is the hero; the reel's job is rhythm and order.

## Rules

- Lead with the strongest piece, end with the second strongest. The middle varies pace and medium.
- Each piece gets a world: its own ground, a frame or device that suits it (a phone for an app, a desk for print, a browser for a site), entered through a cause.
- Cut on the music, change layout on the bar; one hero move per shot inside each piece.
- Real work only, credited as the client allows. Recreated screens labelled.
- Muted loops of individual pieces (for a portfolio page) are 4 to 8 s each, posters cut at a representative frame, capped in height for tall pieces so a column of reels does not run off the page.
- The closing frame rhymes with the opening and carries the mark.

## Tells

A grid of thumbnails zooming in one by one; every piece entering with the same push; music that never changes energy.
