# Kinetic typography and word-beat films

Type carries the argument. The words are the set, the colour fields are the lighting, and the rhythm is the music.

## Forms

- **Word beats.** Three to five single words, each its own full-bleed beat ("Fragmented. Structured. Credible."), then the mark. Brand-colour floods between them as chapter changes.
- **Sentence assembly.** Words gather from scattered positions into a sentence, a key word swaps in a highlight box, the sentence clears in one move.
- **Poster maximalism.** Giant condensed type cropped by the frame, duotone, cuts every 0.3 to 0.8 s, one held beat for the facts that must be read (date, venue).
- **Rotating phrase.** A fixed sentence with one slot that rolls through options and lands on the answer.

## Rules

- Three to seven words per scene. A treatment goes stale after about 6 s: change it or cut.
- Every entrance from `craft/typography.md`, varied within the film.
- Words land on beats; a word meant to be read holds at least one beat.
- Hero words may fill 60 to 90% of the width; one size per set of phrases.
- One accent word per scene at most, and only if it carries meaning.

## Tells

All caps everywhere at weight 800, always centred, always the same letter stagger; a full stop after every two-word phrase; the same highlight shape on every line.
