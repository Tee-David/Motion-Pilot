# Roadmap

Motion Pilot is at 1.0. What comes next, roughly in order:

- **More genre playbooks:** title sequences, event openers, app store previews, onboarding flows.
- **More templates:** a logo-construction template that builds a mark from its SVG parts, and a product-demo template with a real-screen camera.
- **Web export:** turning an engine choreography into CSS keyframes and a phase-switching component automatically.
- **Review:** caption-safe and platform-UI overlays on the phone sheet; contrast measured on rendered frames.
- **Sound:** more synthesised palettes, and beat detection good enough to cut to a supplied track without manual checks.
- **References:** a community library of analysed references, described in writing and linked rather than copied.

Suggestions are welcome as issues.
